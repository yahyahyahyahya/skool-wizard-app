Privacy policy

Skool Wizard Uploader — last updated October 21, 2026

This app is used only by its owner, Yahya Kazi, to manage his own YouTube channel. It signs in with Google to his account alone.

  • Other people: the app does not collect personal data from viewers or anyone else.
